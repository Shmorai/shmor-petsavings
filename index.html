Compare pet products and deals without the guesswork

Pet prices vary a lot between online pet stores, supermarkets, subscription brands, independent pet shops and vet practices. The cheapest-looking deal isn’t always the best value, so our guides show you how to compare properly:

  • Food: compare the cost per day at your pet’s feeding amount, not the price per bag or tin.
  • Insurance: compare the type of cover, limits and excess before the premium.
  • Health care: flea, tick and worm treatments can cost less with a written prescription or a vet health plan.
  • Everyday kit: buy beds, crates and harnesses that fit your pet now and later, so you only buy once.

Frequently asked questions

Is PetSavings free to use?
Yes. Our guides are free. When you buy, you deal directly with the retailer, insurer or local business and agree their terms.
Do you sell pet food or insurance?
No. PetSavings is an independent guide and comparison site. We don’t sell products, arrange insurance or give veterinary advice.
Do you cover the US, UK and Europe?
Yes. We explain where rules differ, such as pet food labelling, insurance regulation, prescriptions and licensing for kennels and home boarders.
Is this veterinary advice?
No. Our guides are general information. If you’re worried about your pet’s health, diet or medication, speak to a vet.
